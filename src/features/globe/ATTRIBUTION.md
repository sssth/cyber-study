# Offline globe land data

`land.json` derives from Natural Earth 1:110m physical land polygons (127 polygon records), rounded to four decimal degrees. No country boundaries are displayed.

Source: https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/110m_physical/ne_110m_land.zip
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Retrieved: 2026-11-14. Natural Earth data is public domain.

The data is bundled by Vite. No online map tiles, image services or personal coordinates are requested over the network.
